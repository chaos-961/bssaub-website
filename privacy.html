Privacy

What a member account holds, who can see it, and what this site knows about a visitor who never signs in, which is nothing.

Last updated 7 September 2026

In short

Browsing this site asks nothing of you. No account, no form, no analytics, no advertising.

Making a member account asks for three things: your name, your email address and a password. The password is held by Google, not by us, and we never see it.

The member list is read by the society committee and by nobody else. Ask us to delete your account and it goes.

01

Who we are

The Business Student Society is the student society of the Suliman S. Olayan School of Business at the American University of Beirut, founded in 1965.

This page covers this website and the member accounts made on it. It does not cover Instagram, an email thread, or an event run with a partner, each of which is handled by whoever runs it.

Reach us at aubbusinesssociety@gmail.com or on Instagram at @businessstudentsociety. Either one is enough to ask what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

02

If you never make an account

Nothing about you is collected. There is no contact form on this site, no newsletter box and no comment field.

There is no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no counter, no session recorder. Nobody is measuring what you read here.

The only thing the site writes into your browser before you sign in is described on the cookies page, and there is one item on that list.

03

What a member account holds

Five fields, and no sixth:

  • Your name, exactly as you type it, because it is what the card shows and what the committee searches by.
  • Your email address, which is how you sign in and how we reach you about the card.
  • A search index built from your name, so a committee member looking you up runs one query instead of downloading the whole list.
  • An expiry date for the membership, set by the committee when the card is issued or renewed.
  • The date the account was made.

Your password is not in that list and never reaches us. Firebase Authentication, which is Google, holds it and checks it; we only ever see that a sign in succeeded.

04

What we do not ask for

No student ID number, no date of birth, no phone number, no address, no payment detail and no photograph.

Nothing is asked for that the card does not need, and the card needs a name and a way to reach you.

05

Who can read it

You can read your own record and correct your own name. You cannot move your own expiry date, and neither can anybody else who is not on the committee.

The committee account can read the member list and set expiry dates. That is enforced by the database rules themselves rather than by the screen, so a modified page cannot get past it.

Nobody else. The list is not sold, shared with a sponsor, handed to a recruiter, or used for anything but the membership card and the society's own communication about it.

06

Sponsors and discounts

A discount is honoured in person, by showing the card. The sponsor sees the card and your name on it, in the way any shop sees a customer.

No sponsor receives your details from us. There is no list handed over, no code that reports back, and nothing that tells us or them which offers you used.

07

Where it is kept

In Google Cloud, through Firebase Authentication and Firestore, in a project only the committee can sign in to. Google is our processor: it stores what we put there and does not use it for anything of its own.

The website itself is static files served by GitHub Pages, which keeps the ordinary server records any web host keeps while serving a page.

08

How long it is kept

While your account exists. A lapsed membership is not a deleted account: the card simply stops showing, and renewing brings it back without retyping anything.

Ask us to delete the account and we delete the record. Email aubbusinesssociety@gmail.com from the address you signed up with.

09

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what we hold about you and send you a copy,
  • correct anything wrong in it,
  • delete your account,
  • stop using it for something you object to.

Lebanon's Law 81 of 2018 on electronic transactions and personal data gives you these rights here, and the GDPR gives you the same ones if you are reading this from Europe. Asking costs nothing and we do not ask why.

10

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The account and committee pages carry a content security policy that blocks any script from an origin that is not ours, and neither page contains an inline script for that policy to have to allow.

The committee screen ships as encrypted code rather than as a readable page, so what it looks like and which address opens it are not published beside the site.

If something goes wrong with data we hold about you, we will tell the people affected rather than hope nobody notices.

11

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the version badge in the footer tells you which build of the site you are reading.
